# Employee Attrition & HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes workforce data to understand employee attrition patterns, engagement levels, satisfaction scores, and retention opportunities.

---

## Business Problem

Employee turnover leads to:

- Increased recruitment costs
- Productivity loss
- Knowledge loss
- Workforce instability

Organizations need data-driven retention strategies.

---

## Objective

Develop a Power BI dashboard that helps HR teams identify attrition drivers and workforce risks.

---

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Excel
- HR Analytics
- Workforce Planning

---

## KPIs

- Attrition Rate %
- Retention Rate %
- Employee Satisfaction Score
- Employee Engagement Score
- Average Tenure

---

## Dashboard Features

### Attrition Analysis
Employee turnover monitoring.

### Workforce Demographics
Employee distribution analysis.

### Engagement Dashboard
Employee engagement trends.

### Promotion Analysis
Career growth evaluation.

### Retention Dashboard
Retention opportunities.

---

## Key Insights

- High attrition departments
- Employee risk groups
- Engagement gaps
- Overtime-related turnover

---

## Recommendations

- Improve career growth programs
- Reduce excessive overtime
- Strengthen employee engagement

---

## Business Impact

Supports strategic workforce planning and employee retention initiatives.
